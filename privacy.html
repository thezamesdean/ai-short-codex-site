AI Short Codex

Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026. AI Short Codex is a personal channel-management tool used for the owner's Tiny Proof Lab and Object Unfolded channels. It is not open for public account registration.

Google and YouTube data access

The tool requests the Google OAuth scope youtube.force-ssl. Google describes this scope as access to see, edit and permanently delete YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions. The broad scope is needed for authorized channel operations, including video management and comment replies; it does not mean that every available action will be taken. The owner's instructions and review requirements apply.

Operations may access the connected channel's identifier and title, video metadata and visibility, video statistics, and comments or captions needed for an authorized task. The initial connection uses a read-only channel identity check. This tool does not request Gmail, Drive, contacts or payment access.

Purpose and AI assistance

Data is used to operate the owner's channels, verify that an action targets the right channel, review publishing status, respond to relevant viewer questions and improve future content. Operational information needed for these tasks may be processed by the owner's AI assistant service. The operator does not sell Google user data or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Provider handling of assistant conversation history is governed by that provider's terms and the owner's account settings.

Storage and sharing

OAuth client credentials and access and refresh tokens are stored locally in a private directory on the owner's Mac with access limited to that operating-system user. Tokens are sent to Google's OAuth and YouTube API services as required for authentication and authorized requests. They are not published on this site or intentionally included in assistant messages.

Channel-operation records, such as video IDs, publication status and task-relevant statistics or comments, may be stored in the local working project and the owner's assistant task history. Only the material needed for the task should be retained or shared. Public uploads and comment replies are disclosed on YouTube only when authorized. This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages; the host may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy policy. The site adds no analytics, advertising scripts or forms.

Retention, control and deletion

Local credentials are retained while the owner wants the connection active. Local operational records are retained while needed to run and review the channels. The owner can request disconnection and removal of locally stored credentials or operational records through the existing operator conversation. Deletion of assistant conversation history or hosting-provider logs is managed through those services' own controls.

The owner can revoke Google access at any time in Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not itself remove previously uploaded YouTube videos or posted comments. Those remain under the channel owner's control.

Google API data policy

AI Short Codex's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and changes

For this personal tool, the owner can use the existing operator conversation or the support contact displayed on the Google authorization screen. This page will be updated if the tool's data practices change. A new use of data or broader access requires the owner's authorization.
